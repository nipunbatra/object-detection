# Object detection lecture: notes for future sessions

- One deck: `index.html` + `content.js` (slides) + `widgets.js` (figure kit and the three live figures) + `lecture.css`, on the shared engine in `shared/` (copied from `generative/lectures/shared`; `deck.js` takes the page title from `meta.series` and drops "optional extension" from Appendix kickers).
- Route: Part 1 why detect (six applications, three hard cases), 2 label to list, 3 boxes and IoU, 4 one box then many, 5 a grid of cells (Andrew Ng's YOLO), 6 NMS, 7 three grids, 8 measuring, wrap-up; then Appendix A (DETR, slides made with `A_()`, `optional`) and Appendix B (`O()`). Optional slides show only with `?all`, in `?reading` and in the PDFs.
- Every displayed number comes from `data/science.json`. Change a number by changing `scripts/science.py` (which asserts what the slides claim), never by typing it into `content.js`. Order: `run_models.py` → `science.py` → `build_notebook.py --run` → `verify.mjs` → `density.mjs` → `contact_sheets.py --all` → `export.mjs`.
- Colours mean something: teal = annotation (made by people), purple = a model's output. The cells, parcel and crack of Part 1 have no COCO class; their boxes are ours (`data/applications.json`) and are always teal.
- Running example: `figures/cat-dog.jpg` (960 × 640). Every photo's sides are multiples of 32 so YOLOv8 runs without resizing and grid cells are photo pixels. Dog GT (407, 83, 768, 588); worked cell (row 10, col 18) at stride 32. Part 1's missed bird (`cat-dog-bird.jpg`, best box IoU 0.913, score 0.098) returns in Part 8.
- Slide helpers: `S_(id, section, title, {builds, body, lines, ask, expected, ...})` with one story line, one question and one expected answer per build; `partBreak(k, …)` opens a part with its route strip, `recap(k, …)` closes it with short numbered lines and a story line that says what comes next. Caveats, sources and teacher cues go in `point`/notes, not on the slide.
- Keep slides light (Nipun’s request, November 2026): the slide body never repeats the story line (the body holds the figure, the numbers and short labels; the line tells the story); split a slide that makes two moves; story lines at most 30 words; a reveal at most 100 words, aiming under 70, not counting code or the part strip; code blocks at most 12 lines. `node scripts/density.mjs` checks the limits and lists the slides worth a look. The Part 1 jobs show the question first, then swap it for the answer with `only()`.
- Write plain sentences (a humanizer pass, November 2026): no slogan fragments such as “Two dogs, two boxes.” or “Fixed size in, any size out.”, no forced groups of three, no “X, not Y” endings, no decorative bold, and no em or en dashes (write “rows 9 to 11”, “2016 and 2017”). Curly quotes and apostrophes are the house style. Body and story line must not paraphrase each other.
- Part 5 follows Andrew Ng's YOLO and stays simple (Nipun, November 2026): sliding windows, his 14 × 14 and 16 × 16 convolutional sliding-window example, a 30 × 20 grid, each cell writing a box in cell units (b_x, b_y inside the cell, b_w, b_h in cells; `S.ng`) and 80 class scores, the centre cell answering for an object. YOLOv8 specifics (four distances, three grids, ten positives per object, the output tensor's columns) live in notes or Appendix B (`opt-ltrb`), not on main-route slides.
- Code slides walk one line per click with `codeSteps([[build, line], ...])`, beside a figure that shows what the line did (IoU, NMS and `detect` in Parts 3 and 6).
- Reveals inside an SVG must use `gat()` / `gonly()` (SVG groups). An HTML `at()` inside an SVG string breaks out of the SVG; `verify.mjs` flags this as "stray content after an SVG".
- Teaching style (Nipun's, see `dl-teaching/generative-modelling-ii/FEEDBACK_LOG.md`): one move per click, one running example with numbers after each equation, results derived not asserted, real model outputs over invented ones (invented values are labelled on the slide), a route map at the start of each part and a recap at the end. The November 2026 rebuild also borrows Andrew Ng's moves (target vector with "?", crop classifier → sliding windows → one conv pass, the centre cell) and Karpathy's (look at the data, print shapes, show failures, the whole detector in a few lines).
- Notebook sections have anchors `part-1` … `part-8` and `part-A`; slide notebook links point at them, and each section asserts its numbers against `science.json`.
- Local preview: `python3 scripts/serve.py` (port 8780, no caching). Models: `~/base/bin/python`; DETR weights in `/Volumes/Extreme SSD/hf-cache` (`HF_HUB_CACHE`, `HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1`); `yolov8n.pt` at `~/yolov8n.pt`, `yolov8l.pt` at `~/git/blog/posts/`.
